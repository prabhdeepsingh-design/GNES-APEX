Web Wizards

Inter-School Web Development Competition — Review the official event guidelines, languages, and technical rules before proceeding to the theme and asset download.

Official Competition Rules

1
A team must comprise 2 participants.
2
Only 1 team is allowed per institution.
3
Participants have to make one website. All the assets will be provided in the competition workspace.
4
The theme will be given on the spot.
5
Time Limit: 90 minutes.
6
Compulsory Languages: HTML & CSS.
7
Optional Language: JavaScript.
8
Permitted Platform: Any offline code/text editor (VS Code, Notepad, Sublime, etc.).
9
Internet access will not be allowed during the competition.
10
Participants are encouraged to bring their own laptops, although computers will be available for use during the event.
11
In case participants are unable to bring laptops, we will provide Notepad and VS Code for their use.
12
Only 1 device is allowed per team.
